Hanoi → Ho Chi Minh City  ·  Planning Document

Vietnam by Bike

A north-to-south touring route on the Soma Wolverine, hotel-to-hotel. Three itineraries — same spine, different pace. The difference is how many train gaps you take and how many detours you earn.

Day-by-Day Itinerary
Logistics & Kit — Soma Wolverine · Hotel-to-Hotel
Best season: Oct–Feb (north cooler, central dry). Avoid May–Oct for the typhoon corridor through the central region.

Bike: The Wolverine is well-suited hotel-to-hotel — faster on the long coastal flats, more rewarding on climbs like Hải Vân and the Đà Lạt approach. 700c fine throughout; pick your line on the rougher DMZ and Ho Chi Minh Road sections.

Navigation: Maps.me offline + Komoot for route building. Vietnamese road signage is inconsistent. Download everything before leaving Hanoi.
Train gaps: Vietnam Railways takes bikes in luggage cars for ~$5–10. Book 48h ahead. Essential tool for leapfrogging the flat, high-traffic Hwy 1 sections without losing days.

Accommodation: Mini-hotels (nhà nghỉ) run $10–20/night, almost always with a room secure enough for the bike. No need to book ahead outside Hội An and Hanoi.

Food & water: Never more than 30 km between roadside stalls. Bánh mì is your long-ride fuel. Iced cà phê sữa at every stop.
⚠ Hydraulic Brake Serviceability
Parts for hydraulic systems are scarce outside Hanoi, Đà Nẵng, and HCMC. Pack accordingly: Bleed kit for your specific system (Shimano mineral oil or SRAM DOT) · Extra fluid (small bottle) · 2 spare pad sets minimum — heat on Hải Vân and the Đà Lạt descent will eat pads faster than flatland riding. A well-maintained hydraulic system rarely fails catastrophically; the risks are pad wear and slow fluid loss, both manageable with this kit.
Bag Setup · No Racks Needed
Hotel-to-hotel you're running light. Frame bag + top tube bag + medium saddle bag carries a 3–4 day kit between laundry stops — all you need. Keep the saddle bag under 3 kg to avoid sway on rough sections. Total carry weight target: under 5 kg.
